
This project is a comprehensive Excel-based **Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize company performance across different product categories, customers, states, and time periods. It automates insights that help make data-driven decisions in real-time.

---

##  Scenario

Imagine you're a business analyst at a mid-sized retail company operating across the United States. The company deals in three major product categories: **Furniture**, **Office Supplies**, and **Technology**.

Management needs a dynamic, user-friendly dashboard that summarizes the **sales trends**, identifies **profitable customers**, monitors **category performance**, and tracks **year-wise and month-wise progress**.

---

##  Business Problem

The sales data is scattered, unorganized, and not providing meaningful insights to guide strategic decisions. The company needs a central tool that can:

- Identify the most profitable products and customers.
- Visualize how each category is performing over time.
- Monitor performance by region and state.
- Analyze customer behavior and purchasing patterns.

---

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**  
- Pivot Tables  
- Pivot Charts  
- Slicers  
- Formulas (`=TEXT(C2, "mmmm")` for extracting month)  
- Conditional Formatting  
- Data Validation  
- Map Charts  

---

## Key Features

- **Automated Calculations**: Sales, profit, and customer summaries calculated dynamically.
- **Interactive Filters**: Slicers for filtering by year and product category.
- **Top Customers Analysis**: Pie chart highlighting customers contributing most to profits.
- **Sales Trends**: Line and area charts showing year-wise and month-wise sales patterns.
- **Geo Analysis**: Sales by state visualized on a map.
- **Product Performance**: Funnel chart comparing product-wise sales.
- **Customer Count per Year**: Bar chart showing engagement trends.

---

##  Sample Business Questions Answered

- Who are our **Top 5 customers** by profit?
- Which **product category** is generating the most sales?
- What is the **sales trend over the last four years**?
- How do **monthly sales** compare throughout the year?
- In which **states** are we seeing the highest sales?
- How has the **customer count** changed each year?

---

## How to Use

1. Download the Excel file.
2. Use the **Year** and **Category** slicers to filter the data.
3. Hover over charts for detailed tooltips.
4. Use pivot filters to explore **top N** customers or products.
5. To update data, simply paste your raw data into the source table — charts and pivot tables will auto-refresh.
